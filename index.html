Methodology: Risk levels are based on changes in attendance, math, and English performance. Students are flagged when declines exceed 5% year over year. Additional flags identify students who are persistently below proficiency even without sharp declines.

i Risk Level Definitions

On Track (0)
No identified risk factors. Student shows healthy attendance (≥90%) and stable or improving academic performance in both subjects.
Early Warning (1)
One risk factor detected. May include attendance concerns OR declining performance in one subject. Recommended interventions: Check-in on Attendance or Math Tutoring or English Support depending on the specific risk driver.
Intervention Needed (2)
Two risk factors present. Student requires targeted support and intervention strategies to prevent further decline.
Critical (3)
All three risk factors identified. Immediate comprehensive intervention required including attendance support and academic assistance in both subjects.
⚠ Stable but Struggling
Student's year-over-year metrics appear stable, but cumulative decline has dropped their estimated absolute level below 70% in Attendance, Math, or English. These students need recovery intervention (credit recovery, intensive tutoring) not just monitoring.
